The approach

Behavioral skills, not just frameworks.

Unlike conventional Design Thinking programmes that primarily teach tools, every programme integrates the behavioral skills that actually make innovation stick — following a four-quadrant learning path.

01

Buy-in

Getting participants' agreement on the topic and helping them understand why it's relevant to their own work.

02

Knowledge

Sharing the relevant concepts, best practices, frameworks, and techniques — the tools, taught with intent.

03

Practice

Participants practice individually and in groups, receiving live feedback from the facilitator and their peers.

04

Application

Participants commit to applying what they've learned back in their actual workplace — not just in the room.

About

A trainer's toolkit, a designer's process.

Sudarshan Pandurang is a design educator, academic leader, corporate trainer, and entrepreneur with over 25 years of experience building and leading design departments, and delivering world-class training to leading corporates across India.

His core competencies — Behavioral Training and Design Thinking — let him bridge people development with innovation challenges: Design Thinking Fundamentals, Human-Centred Design, Service Design, Creativity and Innovation, and Systems Thinking, alongside leadership programs in communication, interpersonal effectiveness, and team building.

His experience spans corporate training at Dale Carnegie and Pearson, and establishing and teaching in design departments at Vogue Institute of Fashion Technology, PES University, DJ Academy of Design, Acharya School of Design, and Nitte University — serving as Head of Department at three of them.

He's a certified Dale Carnegie Trainer, a postgraduate in Geology from IIT Bombay, and holds a Diploma in Design from IDI, Surat plus an industry certificate from NID, Ahmedabad. He is currently building a sustainable design venture crafting handcrafted luminaires from bamboo and cane.
